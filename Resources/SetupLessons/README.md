# Native setup demonstrations

Ten silent recordings from the built MicLine controls on macOS 27, captured on
24 September 2026. Each onboarding stage selects its own chapter: introduction,
permission, microphone, gain, isolation, compression, virtual output, call-app
handoff, reception check and everyday controls after setup.

Clips run for 6–9.2 seconds, use H.264 at 504 × 280 with no audio track, and total
253,572 bytes. Frames were captured at approximately 6–10 fps and encoded at
24 fps; this is instructional footage, not evidence of animation performance.
Camera crops direct attention to the relevant controls. Original captures and
timestamps remain in ignored local evidence.

Playback is muted and loops automatically. Reduce Motion disables autoplay;
native playback controls remain available. Closing the guide stops playback.
Timed native captions describe each action; expandable written steps provide
an equivalent explanation. Intro and everyday-use chapters also appear inline.

Permission footage shows an already-authorized app, not a simulated macOS prompt.
Effect comparisons were recorded with audio stopped. The gain example is a quiet
room, not a target level. Reception must be confirmed by the user in their call
app. BlackHole is an example, not a requirement. No microphone PCM was saved.

Original MicLine recordings, distributed under the repository licence. Replace
a chapter when the controls it teaches change; do not record design mockups.
