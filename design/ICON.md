# Original MicLine icon

Generated with Painter for this project, not based on another application's mark.
Canonical image: `Resources/AppIcon.png`. `AppIcon.iconset/` contains the ten
standard macOS sizes, compiled with `iconutil` into `AppIcon.icns`.

Prompt: “A substantial rounded-square tile in muted deep petrol blue with subtle
enamel depth and soft top lighting. Center a simple sculpted warm-white microphone
capsule with three dark horizontal grille cuts, its stand merging into a short
smooth signal-line curve. Strong recognizable silhouette at 32 pixels, generous
padding, no lettering, no sparkles, no AI symbols, no imitation of existing app
icons. Straight-on macOS Dock icon composition, transparent outside the tile,
small soft contact shadow allowed. Restrained, tactile, precise.”

Painter used native transparency. The PNG alpha channel and transparent corners
were checked, and the icon was inspected composited on light and dark backgrounds.
No Apple design-kit assets are included.
