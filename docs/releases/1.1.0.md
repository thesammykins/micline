# MicLine 1.1.0

## Your microphone, ready when you need it

MicLine resumes your saved virtual-output route after microphone checks and attempts to recover from audio interruptions. After setup, processing starts when the app opens unless you turn that preference off. An explicit microphone pause stays paused across launches; physical listening still requires confirmation.

## Controls that fit your workspace

- Switch between a gain dial and a precision slider without changing your level. Set your preferred gain limits in Settings.
- Use compact mode to keep just the route and meters in view, with a smoother transition back to the full controls.
- Choose System, Light or Dark appearance in Settings.
- See output activity in a small coloured menu-bar meter, or choose monochrome.
- Select your microphone and processed output directly from the menu popover.
- Familiar actions now use icons with hover help and accessibility labels.

## Help where you need it

Gain, low cut and effect order have contextual explanations. Low-cut help lets you compare the filter on and off; listening still uses your confirmed headphone output. Guided setup keeps navigation in place while moving between steps.

Local development builds now explain why production update checking is unavailable. Public releases continue to use the signed automatic-update feed.
