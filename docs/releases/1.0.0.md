# MicLine 1.0.0

The first public release of MicLine for Apple silicon and macOS 27.

## Your microphone, before the call

- Adjust gain and low cut, watch input and output levels, and arrange Audio Unit effects in order.
- Open effect controls, compare with bypass, and listen through an explicitly selected headphone output.
- Send processed audio to an installed virtual device such as BlackHole, then select it in your call or recording app.

## Guided setup

- Check microphone permission and raw input level before choosing an output.
- Try optional Apple Sound Isolation and compression with Keep and Undo controls.
- Follow short, silent guides for each setup stage and confirm reception in your call app.

## Everyday use

- Keep processing with the main window closed and use the menu-bar controls.
- Choose login launch and automatic virtual-route processing independently.
- Receive signed updates through the app's update checker.

## Requirements and limits

- Requires Apple silicon and macOS 27 or later. Virtual audio drivers are installed separately.
- Supports registered Audio Units; compatibility depends on the effect. VST hosting and per-app routing are not included.
